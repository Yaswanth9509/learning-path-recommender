How this works

  1. Describe your goal in plain language.
  2. The profiling engine reads your level, hours, and existing skills.
  3. A gap analysis compares that against everything the goal requires.
  4. The path generator topologically sorts the remaining skills so nothing is ever scheduled before its prerequisites.
  5. Milestones are capped with projects and assessments.
